The teleprompter for creators who talk to camera. Your script scrolls in the middle of the screen while you record with the front camera, so your eyes stay on the lens and your viewers never see you reading.

  • Auto scroll

  • Up to 4K
  • Mirrored text

Support

Got a question, found a bug, or have an idea for Oncam? Write to cookie2000@live.dk.

If you buy Oncam Pro, you can always restore it under Settings → Restore Purchases in the app. You cancel a subscription in Settings → Manage Subscription in the app, or in the App Store under your Apple Account → Subscriptions.

Voice mode needs permission to use the microphone and speech recognition, and recording needs the camera. If you said no by mistake, turn them back on in the iPhone Settings app under Oncam.
