Privacy policy

Last updated 23 October 2026

Oncam is developed by Maya Boelsmand, who is the data controller for the app. This page explains what information is collected when you use the app, and what you can choose yourself.

In short

  • You don't need an account, and we never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your scripts, settings and recordings stay on your iPhone. We have no servers that receive them.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. You decide whether Google may use your data for personalised ads. Oncam Pro removes ads.
  • Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Information on your phone

Scripts you write or paste, and settings such as text size and scroll speed, are stored only on your iPhone. Recordings are kept in temporary storage while you review them and deleted when you discard them or leave the review screen. If you tap Save, the video is added to your Photos library. Everything is deleted when you delete the app, apart from videos you saved to Photos.

Voice mode

Voice mode uses Apple's speech recognition to follow along as you read. When your language supports it, your speech is processed entirely on your iPhone. For other languages, Apple may process the audio on its servers to turn it into text, under Apple's privacy policy. We never receive the audio or the text.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: an optional video you can choose to watch to use Voice mode for one video, and an occasional full-screen ad after recording. Ads never appear while you're reading or recording. The first time an ad is about to show, we ask for your consent with Google's consent message where the law requires it. Oncam doesn't track you across other companies' apps and websites, so iOS never asks for tracking permission and the app doesn't access your device's advertising ID (IDFA).

To show and measure ads, Google may collect your approximate location based on your IP address, information about which ads you see and tap, and technical data about your device and crashes.

You can change your consent at any time under Settings → Ad Privacy Choices in the app. Read more in Google's privacy policy and about how Google uses data from apps. With Oncam Pro, no ads are shown and Google's ad data collection stops.

Purchases and subscriptions

Oncam Pro is paid through Apple. Apple handles the payment and doesn't share your payment details with us. The app only learns whether a purchase is active, so it can unlock Pro features.

Permissions

Oncam only asks for a permission when you use the feature that needs it: the camera and microphone to record you, speech recognition for Voice mode, and adding to Photos to save your takes. Oncam can't see the photos already in your library. You can change permissions at any time in the iPhone Settings app.

Children

Oncam isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their personal information.

Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to access, correct and delete your data, and the right to object, among others. We don't store any personal data about you ourselves, so requests about advertising data are handled by Google. You can also complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet) or the data protection authority where you live.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to contact@theonlyaigency.com.
