SplashDoes the water look good today?

Splash shows over 1,000 Danish beaches and lakes with the water temperature, the weather and a flag that tells you whether heavy rain may have washed sewage into the water. Find the best beach near you, plan a swim with friends and keep track of your swimming season.

  • Water temperature
  • Sewage flag
  • Weather forecast
  • Map
  • Your season
  • Winter swimming

Support

Got a question, found a bug, or have an idea for Splash? Write to contact@theonlyaigency.com.

If you buy Splash Plus, you can always restore it under Settings → Splash Plus → Restore Purchases in the app. You cancel a subscription in the App Store under your Apple Account → Subscriptions.

The flags are Splash's own estimate from rain and sewer overflow outlets, not water samples. Always follow signs and lifeguards at the beach.
